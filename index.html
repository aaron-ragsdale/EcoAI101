Behind the choices

These analogies illustrate principles, not literal energy comparisons. Actual impacts depend on model, workload, hardware, utilization, electricity and cooling. Privacy depends on policy, configuration and authorization.

  • Luccioni, Jernite & Strubell: Power Hungry Processing — task-specific versus general-purpose inference.
  • Google: Measuring the environmental impact of delivering AI at Google Scale — production measurements, not universal per-prompt values.
  • Energy use of AI inference, efficiency pathways, and test-time scaling — workload and reasoning costs.
